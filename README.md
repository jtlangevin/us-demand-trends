# Affordability

### Housing Construction
This dashboard visualizes the economic pressures of new housing development at the county level. By comparing the velocity of new single-family building permits against the average cost of construction, we can identify regional hotspots where growth is either accelerating or becoming prohibitively expensive.

### Fuel Price Comparisons
The spread between electricity and natural gas prices heavily influences electrification economics. This state-by-state analysis highlights where the pricing structure favors natural gas (lower ratio) versus where electricity is more cost-competitive, broken down by residential and commercial sectors.

<iframe src="graphics/fuel_price_ratio_maps.html" width="100%" height="800px" frameborder="0"></iframe>

### Household Energy Burden
Energy burden—the percentage of household income spent on energy bills—is a critical metric for equity and affordability. These maps contrast the *total* energy burden against the *electric-only* burden, illustrating where communities are most vulnerable to price shocks and where electric bills dominate household expenses.

# Load Growth

### Annual Electricity Demand
Tracking annual electricity sales provides a macro view of economic activity and grid utilization. This dashboard details the 5-year and 2-year growth trajectories for total load across the U.S., decomposing the growth into its constituent sectors (Residential, Commercial, Industrial) to reveal the underlying structural drivers.
